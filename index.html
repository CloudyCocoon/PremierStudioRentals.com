Specifications & Design

Our luxury restroom trailers offer a premium experience with upscale amenities, perfect for high-end events and productions. Trusted by major film studios, these trailers bring comfort and elegance to even the most remote movie sets.

Energy

Equipped with solar panels that fully power the trailer during daylight hours for a quiet, eco‑forward experience. Trailers are also fitted with a generator which supplements power at night to ensure uninterrupted comfort and reliability.

Comfort Systems

Year‑round climate control with AC and heat, continuous hot water, and automatic scent diffusion keeps interiors fresh and comfortable in any environment.

Dimensions

Spacious layout measuring 8 ft × 26 ft, offering generous room for guests and premium finishes that complement the most discerning venues.
